# slideworks.app

The landing page for SlideWorks. [`README.md`](README.md) covers running, deploying and editing the page. This file is how the page **pitches**: read it before you change any words a visitor reads.

## Problem first

Every section opens with the presenter's problem, then shows the feature that solves it. The reader should think "I have that problem too" before they see the solution. People know their problems well; they rarely know which solution they want, so a list of features sells less than a problem they recognise.

The problem SlideWorks is built around: a room drifts after about seven minutes. People reach for their phones, and "hands up if you've…" only wakes the few who are happy to be seen. SlideWorks turns the phone they drift to into the way they take part. The `#problem` section near the top states this, and the live-room sections each open with their own slice of it:

- `#present`: speaker notes on a laptop keep you at the podium looking down, and a clicker only moves the slides.
- `#audience`: every step between a guest and your first question loses people.
- `#polls`: "hands up" only hears from a few.
- `#words`: multiple choice only gives back the answers you thought of.
- `#qa`: most questions never get asked.

A new section gets its own problem sentence in the same way.

## What leads

1. **The live room** leads: running the talk from your phone, the room joining with no app or account, polls, word clouds, open text, Q&A and reactions. This is what sets SlideWorks apart.
2. **The agent** comes after, as how the deck gets made. Agents building slides is common, so it supports the pitch rather than leading it.
3. **The presenter brings the substance**: the outline, the numbers and the point of the talk. Copy credits that work. The agent handles layouts, polls on request and the visual check.

Sections show shipped features only. Anything not yet built goes in the roadmap's Next or Planned column, and moves to Ready to use when it ships. Check a claim against `main` of [slide-works/slideworks](https://github.com/slide-works/slideworks) before writing it.

## Words

Write visitor-facing copy to the [Unslop](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/main/pstack/skills/unslop/SKILL.md) rules, which are not installed here:

- Plain words and short sentences, one idea each, in the active voice.
- Separate thoughts with periods and commas. Colons go only before a list.
- Sentence-case headings and straight quotes.
- State what the product does, or a number, instead of how it feels.
- Group things in their natural number rather than threes by habit.
